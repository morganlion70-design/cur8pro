Deep Dive Private Equity

cur8 pro’s Private Equity Thesis

Private Equity (PE) is a staple in institutional investing but remains largely inaccessible for the everyday investor. Institutions favor PE for a simple reason—it consistently delivers above-average returns.

Here’s why: private equity has historically outperformed public markets like the S&P 500 across every major time horizon. This consistent outperformance, coupled with the ability to leverage operational and strategic improvements, makes it a go-to asset class for high-net-worth investors.
